# Awesome-Agricultural-ERP

## Top Agricultural ERP Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Farm Management, Crop & Livestock Tracking, Field Operations, Traceability, Inventory & Agribusiness Resource Planning*

**Last updated: August 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Agricultural ERP** and Farm Management Information Systems (FMIS). These tools help commercial farms, cooperatives, and agribusinesses manage crops, livestock, field activities, inventory, labor, finances, and compliance in one system.



**Examples** include FarmERP, AgriERP, AgVision, Conservis, Produce Pro Software, Traction Ag, AgriWebb, Agworld, Croptracker, and Granular (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: While commercial agricultural ERPs dominate large-scale operations, several mature open-source farm management and ERP systems exist. This section is expanded with every significant active project suitable for self-hosting or customization.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[FarmERP](https://www.farmerp.com/)**  

  Comprehensive agricultural ERP covering the full crop lifecycle, farm operations, traceability, and agribusiness management, with AI-assisted insights (FarmGyan).



- **[AgriERP](https://www.agrierp.com/)**  

  Purpose-built agricultural ERP (often running on Microsoft Dynamics / Business Central or NetSuite) focused on crop farms, orchards, and integrated financials.



- **[AgVision](https://www.agvision.com/)**  

  Agricultural software suite offering ERP-style capabilities for growers, packers, and agribusiness operations.



- **[Conservis](https://conservis.ag/)**  

  Farm management and ERP platform focused on data-driven decision making, field operations, inventory, and financial visibility for large-scale growers.



- **[Produce Pro Software](https://www.producepro.com/)**  

  ERP and operational software tailored for produce growers, packers, shippers, and distributors with strong inventory and traceability features.



- **[Traction Ag](https://www.tractionag.com/)**  

  Farm management platform popular with row-crop and diversified farms for field records, planning, and operational tracking.



- **[AgriWebb](https://www.agriwebb.com/)**  

  Livestock and pasture management platform with strong record-keeping, compliance, mapping, and task management features.



- **[Agworld](https://www.agworld.com/)**  

  Collaborative farm management and agronomy platform used by growers and advisors for planning, work orders, and field records.



- **[Croptracker](https://www.croptracker.com/)**  

  Farm management software focused on specialty crops, labor tracking, food safety, and compliance documentation.



- **[Granular (Corteva)](https://granular.ag/)**  

  Digital farming platform combining field mapping, agronomy insights, operational tracking, and financial tools.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[farmOS](https://github.com/farmOS/farmOS)**  

  Leading open-source web-based farm record-keeping and management application. Supports assets, logs, plans, mapping, sensors, and is actively maintained by a strong community.



- **[Ekylibre](https://github.com/ekylibre/ekylibre)**  

  Open-source Farm Management Information System (FMIS) built with Ruby on Rails and PostGIS. Covers farming operations, accounting links, and geospatial features.



- **[ERPNext Agriculture Domain](https://github.com/frappe/agriculture)**  

  Official agriculture module for ERPNext that adds crop and land management, plant/soil/water analytics, disease and fertilizer tracking on top of a full open-source ERP.



- **[Odoo + AgriOS / Agriculture customizations](https://www.agrios.org/)**  

  Open-source Odoo Community-based solutions and localization efforts (including AgriOS) tailored for agricultural SMEs, cooperatives, and farmer profiling.



- **[Mazao-ERP](https://github.com/HassanMunene/Mazao-ERP)**  

  Modern full-stack open-source ERP aimed at agricultural businesses, covering farmers, crops, inventory, and analytics.



- **[AgriMind.ai & similar dashboards](https://github.com/AMEND09/AgriMind.ai)**  

  Open-source farm management dashboards focused on fields, resources, sustainability metrics, livestock, and reporting.



- **[Specialized / niche farm tools](https://github.com/search?q=farm+management+OR+agriculture+ERP)**  

  Community projects for microgreens, livestock, or specific crop workflows that can complement a broader ERP.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **General ERP foundations**: Full ERPNext or Odoo Community instances customized with agriculture modules and localizations.

- **Mapping & GIS**: Integration with QGIS, PostGIS, and open mapping libraries for field boundaries and precision data.

- **Sensor & IoT**: Open-source stacks (ThingsBoard, Node-RED, etc.) for connecting weather stations, soil sensors, and equipment.

- **Traceability & compliance**: Tools and templates for food safety, organic certification, and lot tracking.

- **Mobile data collection**: KoboToolbox, ODK, or custom mobile forms for field scouting and activity logging.

- Growing number of research and cooperative-driven **FMIS** and **precision agriculture** projects.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Start with **farmOS** for pure farm record-keeping or **ERPNext + Agriculture** / **Odoo** for full ERP capabilities (finance, inventory, HR, CRM).  

Add geospatial capabilities with PostGIS and open mapping tools.  

Integrate IoT/sensor data and mobile data collection for field operations.  

This combination gives complete data ownership and the flexibility to adapt to local crops, regulations, and cooperative structures.
